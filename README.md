## A little more calm. A lot more MyDoc.

Warm paper. Deep graphite. A touch of teal. MyDoc’s **Ink & Seal** design brings considered typography and subtle glass controls to the everyday.

Choose light, dark or pure AMOLED black. Switch between compact file lists and previews. Make yourself at home.

> **Every document. On your device. Yours.**<br>
> Read and work with local files, with document tools that run on your phone.

<br>

## Your next favourite app is a download away.

Get MyDoc from **[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/abhisheksati132/mydoc/releases/latest)**. Open the latest release, download the **universal APK** if you’re unsure which to choose, and install it on your Android device.

Then open a folder, find something worth reading, and settle in.

<sub>PDF · Word · Excel · PowerPoint · EPUB · Text · Images · Audio · Video<br>Office documents are available for viewing; layout support varies by format.</sub>
